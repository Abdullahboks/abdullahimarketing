For optimal results, I recommend reaching out 30 to 60 days before your intended publication date. This allows ample time for sales copy optimization, ARC team management, visual asset creation, and promo site booking.

However, I guarantee professional execution, data-optimized targeting, high-converting assets, and strategic management designed to maximize your campaign's revenue potential.

Yes! You can hire me for full launch campaigns or individual stand-alone services such as Book Description Sales Copy, 3D Mockups, Book Trailer Production, or Amazon Ads setup.
